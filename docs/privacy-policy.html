Privacy Policy for Movie Deck

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Movie Deck ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

No account required

Movie Deck works without creating an account. You can use all core features anonymously.

Data we collect

  1. Watchlist data (on your device): Movies you save, mark as seen, and your preferences are stored on your device. We do not sell or share this data.
  2. Anonymous identifier: To process shared videos in the background, the app creates a random anonymous identifier on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any personal information.
  3. Shared video links: When you share a YouTube Short, TikTok, or Instagram Reel to Movie Deck, the video URL is sent to our servers to extract movie titles. We do not store the video content itself.
  4. Push notification token: If you enable notifications, your device's push token is stored so we can notify you when your videos finish processing. You can disable this anytime in your device settings.
  5. Movie data (TMDB): The app fetches public movie information (titles, posters, ratings, descriptions) from The Movie Database (TMDB). No personal data is sent to TMDB.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or photos. We do not use advertising SDKs and do not sell data to third parties.

Data retention

Shared video links are deleted from our servers after processing. Your watchlist remains on your device until you delete the app.

Children

Movie Deck is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
