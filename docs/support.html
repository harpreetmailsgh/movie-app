Need help with Save My Movie? Email us at aitbconsulting@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

How do I save movies from a Reel or Short?

Share the video to Save My Movie from YouTube, TikTok or Instagram — or paste the video link on the Add screen. The movie titles are found automatically and added to your watchlist.

The app didn't find a movie in my video

The app looks for movie titles in the video. If nothing is found, try sharing a video that names the movies clearly, and check that the link is a YouTube Short, TikTok or Instagram Reel.

How do I watch a trailer?

Open any movie in your watchlist and tap Trailer.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account & data. This deletes your watchlist, feedback, queued videos and push token from our servers and clears your saved movies from this phone.
